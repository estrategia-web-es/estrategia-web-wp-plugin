How to maintain: they were downloaded from jsDelivr and cleaned up (only
viewBox and <path>, no title or colour: they are painted with currentColor).
A new icon: a <slug>.svg file here with the same shape, its line above with
its licence (only CC0, MIT, GPL or compatible) and its name in
Icons::labels() (includes/class-icons.php).
